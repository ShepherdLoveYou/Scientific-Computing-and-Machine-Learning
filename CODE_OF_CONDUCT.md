# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

本项目采用 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-cn/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 作为贡献者与社区成员行为准则的蓝本。

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as the basis for contributor and community behavior.

---

## 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

为营造开放、友好、多元、包容的学习与协作环境，我们——作为贡献者与维护者——承诺无论年龄、体型、可见与不可见的残疾、族裔、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济地位、国籍、个人外表、种族、宗教、性取向和性身份如何，所有在社区中参与的人都不会受到骚扰。

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

---

## 我们的标准 / Our Standards

**积极的行为 / Positive behavior**

- 展示同理心和友善 / Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- 尊重不同的观点和经验 / Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- 建设性地提出与接受反馈 / Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- 为错误负责并致歉 / Accepting responsibility and apologising for mistakes
- 以整个社区为先考虑 / Focusing on what is best for the community

**不可接受的行为 / Unacceptable behavior**

- 性化的语言或图像 / Sexualised language or imagery
- 挑衅、侮辱、贬低性评论、人身或政治攻击 / Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- 公开或私下骚扰 / Public or private harassment
- 未经明确许可发布他人私人信息 / Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- 在专业场合合理视为不当的其他行为 / Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## 执行 / Enforcement

报告辱骂、骚扰或其他不当行为可通过仓库 issue 或直接联系维护者。所有投诉都会被及时、公平地审阅与调查。

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior via the repo issues or by contacting a maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
